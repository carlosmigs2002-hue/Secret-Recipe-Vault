Quick answers

Before you unlock the vault.

Who is this book for?
Anyone who wants faster, cheaper, better versions of the food they already crave — dessert hacks, copycat fast food, coffee-shop drinks, and easy dinners, without a culinary degree.
Do I need any special equipment?
Mostly no. Most recipes use a microwave, a mug, or a stovetop. A handful of chapters (like Air Fryer Food Hacks) call for an air fryer, but those are clearly labeled.
Are the wellness recipes a replacement for medical advice?
No. The Wellness Kitchen collection is educational and doesn't replace care from your doctor — several recipes include a note to check with a clinician first.

An instant digital download in PDF format, readable on your phone, tablet, or computer right after checkout.
